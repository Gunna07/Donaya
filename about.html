BEAUTY • TRAVEL • FREEDOM

Hi, I'm Donaya, founder and CEO of Crown & Contour Studios and Crown & Contour Escapes.

This brand was born from a clear, unwavering vision: helping women look, feel, and live their absolute best. I started in the beauty and luxury hair industry with Crown & Contour Studios, selecting every single extension and beauty essential with quality, confidence, and elegance in mind.

As ambitious women, we pour so much of our energy into our careers, families, and businesses. When it’s time to take a break and explore the world, the last thing we should have to do is stress over logistics, compare endless flight prices, or referee complicated group chats.

That is why I created Crown & Contour Escapes. To give women the ultimate VIP luxury experience where every detail is curated from start to finish. I handle the flights, 5-star hotels, transfers, and private excursions—so all you do is show up, sip champagne, and celebrate life.
